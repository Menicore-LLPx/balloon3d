Our Technology

State-of-the-art technologies delivering high-precision, industrial-grade solutions tailored to your needs.

FDM

Cost-effective printing using thermoplastic filaments for prototypes and functional parts.

  • → Affordable prototyping
  • → Wide material options
  • → Strong functional parts
01

SLS

Powder-based printing that creates strong, durable, and complex geometries without support structures.

  • → No support structures
  • → High strength
  • → Complex geometries
02

SLA

High-precision resin printing for smooth surfaces and fine details.

  • → High accuracy
  • → Smooth surface finish
  • → Ideal for detailed models
03

Polyjet

Advanced multi-material printing with high resolution and smooth finishes.

  • → Multi-material capability
  • → High resolution
  • → Smooth texture
04

Multicolor

Full-color 3D printing for realistic models, prototypes, and presentation pieces.

  • → Realistic color output
  • → Visual prototypes
  • → Marketing models
05

3D Scan

Accurate digital capture of physical objects for reverse engineering and replication.

  • → High precision scanning
  • → Reverse engineering
  • → Digital archiving
