FDM Printing

Rapid, cost-effective prototyping using engineering-grade thermoplastics. Ideal for proof-of-concept and large functional parts.

  • Cost-effective

High-detail, smooth surface finish printing. Perfect for intricate designs, jewelry casting, and presentation models.

  • Injection mold-like finish
